# Concept 1 Image Prompts

All Concept 1 image assets were created with the built-in image generation tool and saved into this self-contained concept directory. Each prompt intentionally avoids readable text, logos, watermarks, fake brand marks, and surreal artifacts.

## hero-digital-operations.png

Output mode: generated

Saved path: `concepts/01-digital-operations-partner/assets/img/hero-digital-operations.png`

Prompt:

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: website hero image for OZMO Digital
Primary request: Warm realistic editorial photograph of a small business owner and a digital consultant reviewing a calm digital operations dashboard on a laptop in a natural-light office.
Scene/backdrop: Midwest service-business office, practical and trustworthy.
Style/medium: photorealistic editorial website imagery.
Composition/framing: horizontal image with clear subject focus and enough quiet negative space for page layout.
Lighting/mood: warm natural light, calm, capable, premium but not corporate.
Color palette: navy, warm cream, terracotta accents.
Constraints: no readable text, no logos, no watermark, no surreal elements.
Avoid: cold corporate stock styling, sci-fi UI, fake brand marks.
```
