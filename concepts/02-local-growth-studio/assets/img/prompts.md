# Concept 2 Image Prompts

All Concept 2 image assets were created with the built-in image generation tool and saved into this self-contained concept directory. Each prompt intentionally avoids readable text, logos, watermarks, fake brand marks, and surreal artifacts.

## hero-local-growth.png

Output mode: generated

Saved path: `concepts/02-local-growth-studio/assets/img/hero-local-growth.png`

Prompt:

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: website hero image for OZMO Digital Concept 2
Primary request: Warm realistic editorial photograph of a local service-business owner and a digital advisor reviewing a friendly website and local growth plan in a bright small-town office or storefront.
Scene/backdrop: community-aware Midwest business setting.
Style/medium: photorealistic editorial website imagery.
Composition/framing: horizontal image with a human advisory moment and enough quiet workspace detail for a premium homepage hero.
Lighting/mood: warm natural light, trustworthy, local, capable.
Color palette: navy, cream, and terracotta accents.
Constraints: no readable text, no logos, no watermark, no surreal elements.
Avoid: cold corporate stock styling, fake brand marks, overproduced agency scenes.
```
